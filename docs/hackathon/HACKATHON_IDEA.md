# EduMemory

**Tagline:** A lifelong AI-powered learning passport and opportunity network.

## Problem Statement

Students spend years building skills through classroom projects, STEM clubs, competitions, certifications, reflections, and informal learning. When they apply for colleges, technical schools, scholarships, internships, fellowships, competitions, and nonprofit programs, they often forget the experiences that prove they are qualified.

Teachers also struggle to see a complete picture of student growth across months and years. Evidence is scattered across assignments, LMS posts, photos, notebooks, feedback, and conversations.

## Solution

EduMemory extends AI Inquiry Companion with a student-owned learning passport. The system observes classroom learning, identifies growth, creates evidence summaries, routes achievements to teachers for verification, and preserves verified credentials in a portable portfolio.

For the Sui Overflow hackathon demo:

- Sui is represented by mock verifiable credentials and student ownership.
- Walrus is represented by mock long-term evidence storage for reflections, photos, videos, notebooks, and artifacts.
- The focus is a compelling real-world workflow rather than production blockchain infrastructure.

## Target Users

- **Students:** Need a portable record of achievements, skills, reflections, and evidence.
- **Teachers:** Need a lightweight way to verify student growth and recommend credentials.
- **Families:** Need clearer visibility into progress and opportunities.
- **Schools and districts:** Need longitudinal evidence of STEM learning and career readiness.
- **Scholarship and opportunity providers:** Need better signals of student readiness beyond grades.

## Market Opportunity

EduMemory sits at the intersection of K-12 AI, digital credentials, student portfolios, career readiness, STEM pathways, and opportunity matching. Schools already invest in LMS platforms, student information systems, career planning tools, and assessment products, but few systems preserve verified learning evidence over time in a student-owned format.

A practical learning passport could support:

- STEM programs and career academies
- CTE pathways
- Scholarship preparation
- Competition portfolios
- College and internship readiness
- District-level evidence of durable skill development

## Why This Matters

The students with the most potential are not always the students with the best ability to document and explain their growth. EduMemory helps students remember what they have built, understand who they are becoming, and connect that growth to future opportunities.

The result is not just a better portfolio. It is a more equitable bridge between classroom learning and real opportunity.
