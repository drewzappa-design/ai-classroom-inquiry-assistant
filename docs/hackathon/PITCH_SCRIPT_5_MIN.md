# EduMemory 5-Minute Pitch Script

## 0:00-0:30 - Opening

EduMemory is a lifelong AI-powered learning passport and opportunity network.

The problem is simple but painful: students spend years building skills, winning awards, completing projects, joining STEM clubs, and becoming eligible for opportunities. But when they apply for scholarships, internships, academies, fellowships, or colleges, they often forget the evidence that proves they are qualified.

Teachers see the growth, but the evidence is scattered across assignments, reflections, notebooks, photos, and conversations.

## 0:30-1:10 - Product Vision

EduMemory changes that.

It adds an AI agent to an existing classroom inquiry assistant. The agent does not replace the student's thinking. It watches for evidence of real learning, documents growth, recommends achievements, and routes them to a teacher for verification.

Once verified, the achievement becomes a student-owned credential, and the learning evidence becomes part of a durable memory record.

## 1:10-2:10 - Demo Student

In this demo, our student is Maya Rodriguez, a 7th grade engineering student.

Maya completed a wind-resistant tower design challenge. Her reflection says that her first tower bent when the fan turned on, so she changed the base to a triangle, shortened the top section, and added cross braces.

This is not just a student answer. It is evidence of engineering growth.

Click **Analyze Growth**.

The EduMemory Agent identifies three important signals:

- Maya completed multiple design iterations.
- She improved her use of engineering constraints.
- She provided evidence-based reasoning.

The agent shows a growth trend: at the beginning, Maya needed support identifying constraints. Now she independently applies constraints to improve designs.

The agent confidence score is 96%, and the recommendation is to issue Engineering Design Level 1.

## 2:10-2:55 - Walrus Learning Memory

Click **Create Learning Memory**.

EduMemory creates a Walrus Learning Memory with ID `WALRUS-2026-ENG-0001`.

This memory preserves:

- Student reflection
- Design notebook
- Prototype images
- Teacher feedback
- AI growth analysis

This matters because Maya's evidence is no longer trapped inside one assignment, one teacher's notes, or one school year. It becomes a durable part of her learning passport.

## 2:55-3:40 - Teacher Verification And Sui Credential

Now go to the Teacher view.

The teacher sees a pending achievement recommendation for Maya: Engineering Design Level 1.

Click **Review Evidence**.

The teacher can inspect the reflection, the AI analysis, and the artifact evidence. The teacher remains the verifier. EduMemory does not automatically award credentials without educator approval.

Click **Approve Engineering Design Level 1**.

Now EduMemory issues a mock Sui credential. In production, Sui would represent a verifiable, student-owned achievement record. The credential belongs to Maya and can travel beyond a single classroom, school, or district.

## 3:40-4:25 - Portfolio And Opportunity Matching

Open the Portfolio view.

Maya now has a verified learning credential for Engineering Design Level 1, along with robotics examples, TSA examples, and an evidence history.

Now open Opportunities.

EduMemory does not just list programs. It explains why Maya matches.

NASA STEM Fellowship is a 92% match based on engineering design achievements, iterative problem solving, and STEM engagement.

Engineering Summer Academy is an 87% match based on project portfolio, teacher verified skills, and growth trajectory.

Robotics Leadership Scholarship is a 95% match based on technical achievements, leadership evidence, and long-term STEM participation.

## 4:25-5:00 - Closing

EduMemory is built for the Agentic Web because the AI takes action across a real workflow:

It observes, analyzes, recommends, preserves, and matches.

Sui gives the student ownership of verified credentials.

Walrus gives the student durable memory for evidence and artifacts.

The core idea is this:

AI did not replace the student's thinking. AI documented the student's growth and opened new opportunities.

EduMemory can help students carry their learning story with them for life.
