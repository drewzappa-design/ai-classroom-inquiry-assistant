# EduMemory Demo Script

## Demo Student

**Maya Rodriguez**  
7th Grade Engineering Student

## Storyline

Maya completes an engineering design challenge. The AI notices that she improved in using constraints and iterating on her design. EduMemory creates an evidence summary. The teacher receives an achievement recommendation and approves **Engineering Design Level 1**. A mock Sui credential is created, Maya's portfolio updates, and the system recommends future opportunities.

## Screen Sequence

### 1. Start On The Existing App

**Voiceover:**  
"AI Inquiry Companion already supports classroom inquiry and reflection. For Sui Overflow, we added EduMemory Demo Mode as an additive feature that preserves the current teacher and student workflows."

**Click:**  
`EduMemory Demo`

### 2. Student View: Maya's Profile

**Screen shows:**

- Maya Rodriguez
- 7th Grade Engineering Student
- Current focus: Engineering design, robotics, TSA preparation
- Recent activity: Wind-resistant tower challenge

**Voiceover:**  
"Maya is a seventh-grade engineering student. EduMemory gives her a learning passport that grows as she completes projects, reflects, and demonstrates skills."

### 3. Student View: Engineering Reflection

**Click:**  
`Student`

**Sample student response:**  
"My first tower was tall but it bent when the fan turned on. I changed the base to a triangle, shortened the top section, and used cross braces. The second version held more weight because I thought about wind and material limits before rebuilding."

**Voiceover:**  
"Maya's reflection includes evidence of constraints, testing, iteration, and design reasoning."

### 4. Student View: AI Growth Analysis

**Click:**  
`Analyze Growth`

**Sample AI response:**  
"Maya shows growth in engineering design. She identified constraints, tested a prototype, diagnosed failure, and improved the structure through iteration. Recommended evidence summary: Engineering Design Level 1."

**Voiceover:**
