# EduMemory Final Q&A Cards

## Card 1 - What is EduMemory?

EduMemory is a lifelong AI-powered learning passport and opportunity network.

It uses an AI agent to document student growth, preserve evidence, support teacher verification, and connect learners to future opportunities.

## Card 2 - What problem are you solving?

Students forget years of awards, skills, projects, competitions, and eligibility evidence.

EduMemory helps students carry proof of growth beyond one assignment, classroom, school, or platform.

## Card 3 - What is the main message?

AI did not replace the student's thinking. AI documented the student's growth and opened new opportunities.

## Card 8 - Does AI award credentials automatically?

No.

The AI recommends based on evidence. A teacher verifies before a credential is approved.

## Card 9 - Why not just use a database?

A database is controlled by one institution or app.

Students need portable, verifiable, student-owned learning evidence that can survive beyond one school, district, or platform.
